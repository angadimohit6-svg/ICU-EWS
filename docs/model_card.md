## Intended use

- **Intended for:** research demonstrations, teaching, dashboard prototyping, reproducibility checks and
  methodological review of an end-to-end early-warning pipeline.
- **Not intended for:** patient triage, clinical decision support, escalation or treatment decisions, resource
  allocation, benchmarking hospitals, or any use with real patient records without separate ethics approval,
  validation and regulatory review.

## Training data

The model is trained on a synthetic cohort produced by `icu_ews/data/demo.py`:

- 420 pseudonymous patients, 14,901 hourly observation rows, 1,527 laboratory panels.
- 83 documented deterioration events (19.8%).
- Admission types: medical, surgical, respiratory, cardiac, neurological.
- Outcomes are generated by a latent severity process that also drives physiological trajectories, so the model
  learns the simulator's structure. This is a demonstration of methodology, not a clinical discovery.

No MIMIC-IV, eICU, hospital or other real patient data is used. No PHI is present in the artifact.

## Evaluation data

- Patient-level hold-out partition: 126 patients, 25 events (19.8%).
- Training partition: 294 patients.
- 5-fold cross-validation on the training partition only.
- Test partition is used once, after model selection and threshold selection.

## Explainability

- Global importance: mean absolute SHAP value per feature on held-out assessments.
- Beeswarm: distribution of signed contributions across assessments.
- Dependence: SHAP value versus feature value for a selected input.
- Individual waterfall: additive breakdown for one assessment, including increasing and decreasing contributors.

Top contributors by mean absolute SHAP in this artifact include C-reactive protein, lactate, 8-hour heart-rate
mean, white cell count and 8-hour SpO₂ mean. These rankings reflect this synthetic model and are not causal.

## Limitations

- Synthetic training and evaluation data; no external or prospective validation.
- Small test sample: 126 patients / 25 events.
- A single 24-hour horizon and a single assessment anchor per patient.
- Calibration is specific to the synthetic outcome distribution.
- No fairness audit across demographic subgroups with adequate statistical power.
- No drift monitoring, silent-mode deployment or clinician workflow evaluation.
- Risk bands are configurable display cut-offs, not validated clinical thresholds.

## Ethical considerations

- The model must not be used to make or defer clinical decisions.
- False negatives could delay escalation; false positives could contribute to alarm fatigue. Any real-world use
  requires prospective evaluation, governance, monitoring and clinician oversight.
- The artifact contains synthetic data only. Adapting the pipeline to credentialed datasets requires compliance
  with the relevant data-use agreements; do not redistribute restricted data or models derived from it without
  authorization.
- UI copy labels all predictions as model output, not diagnosis, and marks the workspace as a demonstration.

## Reproducibility

```bash
python scripts/bootstrap_demo.py --seed 7 --patients 420 --slug demo-cohort-v1
```

The command regenerates the cohort, validates the schema, retrains all three models, recomputes cross-validation
and held-out metrics, fits the explainability inputs and rewrites the artifact bundle. Evaluation numbers in the
dashboard are read from that bundle and are recomputed from saved probabilities at runtime.

## Citation

If you reference this prototype, describe it as a research demonstration built on a synthetic cohort; do not cite
the metrics as clinical evidence.
