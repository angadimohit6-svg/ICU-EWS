# Data dictionary — ICU-EWS demo cohort

All data in `artifacts/bundles/demo-cohort-v1/` is synthetic, generated by `icu_ews/data/demo.py`
(`demo-cohort/1.2`, seed `7`). Identifiers are pseudonymous. No real patient information is present.

## Derived scores

### MEWS

The Modified Early Warning Score follows the five-parameter Subbe et al. (2001) table and is computed only from
recorded observations. The total is reported only when all five components are present.

| Component | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heart rate (bpm) | — | < 40 | 41–50 | 51–100 | 101–110 | 111–129 | ≥ 130 |
| Systolic BP (mmHg) | ≤ 70 | 71–80 | 81–100 | 101–199 | — | ≥ 200 | — |
| Respiratory rate (/min) | — | < 9 | — | 9–14 | 15–20 | 21–29 | ≥ 30 |
| Temperature (°C) | — | < 35.0 | — | 35.0–38.4 | — | ≥ 38.5 | — |
| AVPU | — | — | — | A | V | P | U |

The table reflects the implemented score ranges (`icu_ews/data/mews.py`).

### Risk bands

Predicted probabilities are mapped to display bands with the session thresholds (defaults):

| Band | Default cut-off |
|---|---|
| Critical | ≥ 75% |
| High | ≥ 55% |
| Moderate | ≥ 35% |
| Low | < 35% |

These cut-offs are display parameters and are not validated clinical thresholds. They can be changed in Settings
and never alter the trained model or saved evaluation metrics.

## Derived alerts

Alerts are deterministic functions of the snapshot and the active thresholds. They are local research records
only and are never transmitted to a clinical system.

| Rule | Category | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| `spo2_critical` | Critical | SpO₂ < 88% |
| `spo2_high` | High | SpO₂ 88–91.9% |
| `sbp_critical` | Critical | Systolic BP < 85 mmHg |
| `sbp_high` | High | Systolic BP 85–99.9 mmHg |
| `hr_critical` | Critical | HR > 140 or < 40 bpm |
| `hr_high` | High | HR 121–140 bpm |
| `hr_moderate` | Moderate | HR 111–120 bpm |
| `rr_critical` | Critical | RR ≥ 32 /min |
| `rr_high` | High | RR 25–31 /min |
| `rr_moderate` | Moderate | RR 21–24 /min |
| `temp_high` | High | Temperature ≥ 39.5 °C |
| `temp_moderate` | Moderate | Temperature 38.5–39.4 °C |
| `temp_low` | High | Temperature < 35.0 °C |
| `mews_critical` | Critical | MEWS ≥ 7 |
| `mews_high` | High | MEWS 5–6 |
| `mews_moderate` | Moderate | MEWS 4 |
| `avpu_critical` | Critical | AVPU level `P` or `U` |
| `lactate_high` | High | Lactate ≥ 4.0 mmol/L |
| `lactate_moderate` | Moderate | Lactate 2.0–3.9 mmol/L |
| `creatinine_moderate` | Moderate | Creatinine ≥ 2.0 mg/dL |
| `model_critical` | Critical | Predicted risk ≥ critical threshold |
| `model_high` | High | Predicted risk ≥ high threshold |
| `data_gap` | Informational | Data completeness < 70% |

Acknowledgement and resolution state is stored locally keyed by alert ID and can be cleared from Settings.
